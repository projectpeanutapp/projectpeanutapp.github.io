Peanut is operated by Fan Zhang. Your expenses, amounts, dates, categories, and account and payment labels are stored on your device. Peanut requires no account, does not connect to banks, and has no in-app advertising, tracking, or automatic cloud sync. When you create a ledger, you can choose CNY, USD, CAD, JPY, HKD, TWD, or KRW. Your ledger uses one fixed currency. Existing yuan ledgers remain in CNY, with no currency conversion.

You can manually save a full JSON backup to a location you choose in Files or export a monthly Excel file. These files are readable, so store them securely. SHA-256 checks backup integrity. If you choose iCloud Drive or another provider, that provider manages sync and retention. You can preview a backup before confirming replacement of your ledger. A backup in another currency cannot replace it. Some migrations or restores preserve a local copy of the previous ledger. Deleting an expense leaves earlier backups and exports intact. Removing the app removes its on-device data. Manage saved copies separately. There is no separate deletion or automatic cleanup for internal recovery copies. iOS manages device backups according to your settings.

Our public pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical information to serve them. If you email us, we receive your address, message, and any attachments you choose to send through Outlook. We use this information only to provide support and troubleshoot problems. We may use AI to help translate, giving preference to issue summaries with identifying details removed. We do not send full ledgers or unredacted private attachments to AI tools. After resolving an issue, we delete copies of the email and attachments that we control, except where necessary for legal or security reasons. Service providers handle copies they control under their own practices. Write to hello.peanut@outlook.com from your original email address to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the support data we hold.
